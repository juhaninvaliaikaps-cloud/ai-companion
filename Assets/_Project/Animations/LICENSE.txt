Idle, Talk, Happy, Sad, Surprised, and Thinking are original Humanoid muscle
curves made for this repository. They were written for this project. They are
not Mixamo clips, not Quaternius clips, and not clips from Free Test Character
Asuna (MSGDI, Unity Asset Store).

Swap a later Humanoid clip by replacing the .anim file, or by dropping the new
clip onto that state in Companion.controller. Keep third-party clips whose
licence does not allow redistribution out of this public repository.
